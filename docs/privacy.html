HealthBridge Privacy Policy

HealthBridge is a personal app used only by its developer.

What data it accesses

With your permission, it reads your activity, health metrics, sleep and nutrition data from the Google Health API (read-only).

How the data is used

The data is written directly into Apple Health on your own iPhone. It is not sent to any server, not stored anywhere else, not shared with or sold to anyone, and not used for advertising.

Optional food logging: a meal photo or description you choose to analyze is sent to Anthropic's Claude API using your own API key; a scanned barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts. Google Health data is never sent to either.

Storage

The only thing the app stores is a Google sign-in token, kept in the iPhone Keychain. Disconnecting in the app deletes it. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

HealthBridge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
